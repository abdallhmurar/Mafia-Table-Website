PRIVACY / PLAYER INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Mafia Table — Last updated: November 12, 2026

In short: Mafia Table uses Epic Online Services (EOS) to sign players in and create or join multiplayer rooms. According to the game developer, Mafia Table does not maintain its own database of players' account details, chat conversations, or match results. Epic Games, Microsoft, and the website hosting provider may separately process information under their own privacy notices.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes data handling for the Mafia Table Windows game and its official informational website at mafiatable.duckdns.org. Mafia Table is available free of charge through the Microsoft Store.

2. Information used for multiplayer

When you choose to use online features, the game connects to Epic Online Services for authentication and multiplayer room functionality. To provide these features, the game may access and use the Epic account information authorized in the Epic consent screen, including:

  • Epic account identifier and display name, along with basic profile information permitted by Epic.
  • Authentication status and temporary access credentials needed for a signed-in session.
  • Online room (lobby) identifiers, membership and related session data needed to create, find, and join rooms.

The game developer states that the game does not operate a separate player-account database or intentionally store player chat histories or match results. Necessary information may be held temporarily while a session is running. The game does not ask players to provide their Epic password or payment details to the developer.

3. How information is used

Player information accessed by the game is used to authenticate players, display the permitted player identity during online play, and support the creation, discovery and joining of multiplayer rooms. It is not used by the developer for a separate advertising profile or sold by the developer.

4. Third-party services

Epic Online Services provides the sign-in and multiplayer services. Microsoft Store distributes the Windows application. Each service may handle technical data, identifiers, and operational records to provide its services according to its own terms and privacy practices. Refer to the Epic Games Privacy Policy and Microsoft Privacy Statement. This policy does not attempt to define Epic's or Microsoft's independent data retention periods or processing practices.

5. Website and hosting

The Mafia Table website is a static informational site hosted using Vercel and backed by a public GitHub repository. It does not provide player login or an online-game account form. Hosting providers can process standard website access logs, including IP addresses and browser/request information, to serve the site and keep it secure. For details, consult the Vercel Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Storage, retention, access and deletion

According to the developer, Mafia Table itself has no separate stored player-profile records, chat histories or match-result database to retain or delete. Authentication and lobby information is used for gameplay sessions. Information retained by Epic Games or Microsoft is controlled under their respective policies. To stop sharing Epic account information with the game, players may revoke the game's access from their Epic account's connected apps settings. For account information maintained by Epic or Microsoft, use the relevant provider's privacy tools and support channels.

7. Contact for privacy inquiries

For privacy questions, account-access concerns, or requests about data handled directly by Mafia Table, please contact the game developer at kepleralquds@gmail.com or abdallhmurar@gmail.com. You can also consult the Microsoft Store listing for further publisher information. Please do not post account IDs, tokens or other private information in public GitHub issues. For information held independently by Epic Games or Microsoft, use their privacy support channels.

8. Changes

If the game adds new functionality such as saved accounts, chat storage, analytics or in-game purchases, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes. The date at the top will indicate the latest version.
